Established in 2014, Bridge Group Solutions originated as a specialized technology outsourcing and engineering firm in India. Over more than a decade of disciplined execution, BGS has expanded into an international digital transformation partner, delivering enterprise software, ERPs, CRM platforms, and data systems to organizations across 30+ countries worldwide with corporate desks in Delhi NCR and Mumbai.
We engineer mission-critical systems across diverse high-complexity industries, including Financial Technology & Quantitative Asset Management, Healthcare & Telehealth, Supply Chain & Global Logistics, E-Commerce & Retail, Educational Technology, Manufacturing ERP, and Enterprise Cloud SaaS.
We operate an integrated global delivery model pairing every client with dedicated technical account managers who provide real-time overlap during your business day (covering US EST/PST, UK GMT, European CET, and Australia AEST). Asynchronous sprint summaries, automated CI/CD builds, and continuous communication portals keep project momentum moving around the clock.
Unlike conventional transactional agencies or template-based shops, BGS operates as an architectural co-pilot. We enforce senior developer staffing ratios, require senior architect reviews on every pull request, build strictly bespoke zero-compromise codebases, and stake our reputation on contractual SLA delivery velocity.
Our governance combines deep institutional pedigree in quantitative finance, artificial intelligence, defense systems, and tech venture scaling. Our engineering ethos is anchored in four core values: Purpose-Driven Architecture, Future-Ready Scalability, High-Velocity Execution, and Uncompromising System Reliability.
We support companies at every growth inflection point. For early-stage venture-backed startups, we build lean, investor-ready MVPs with high iteration velocity. For scaling mid-market enterprises and Fortune 500 corporations, we deliver robust enterprise ERPs, high-throughput microservices, comprehensive security compliance, and dedicated engineering pods.
