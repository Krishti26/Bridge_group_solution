Bridge Group Solutions (BGS) enforces strict controls on the handling of all personal and sensitive information. This Privacy & Data Protection Policy outlines our commitment to safeguarding information and defines the responsibilities of all individuals interacting with our systems and services.

01 Purpose and Scope

This policy applies to all individuals whose data is collected, processed, or stored by BGS, including employees, clients, contractors, service providers, and users of our websites and digital services. It defines our principles, responsibilities, and measures to ensure the privacy, security, and ethical handling of personal data throughout its lifecycle.

02 Legal and Regulatory Compliance

BGS complies with applicable Indian laws (Information Technology Act, 2000 and DPDP Act, 2023) and, where relevant, international laws such as the EU GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). Compliance ensures lawful processing and upholds individual rights globally.

03 Data Retention and Disposal

Data is retained only as long as necessary for its intended purpose or to meet legal obligations. Once expired, it is securely deleted or anonymized to prevent unauthorized recovery.

04 Third-Party Data Sharing

We share data only with trusted third-party partners under binding agreements that require compliance with strict data protection standards. Shared data is limited to what is necessary for the defined purpose.

05 Data Breach Response

In the event of a data breach, we will investigate promptly, contain risks, notify affected individuals and authorities where required, and implement corrective measures to prevent recurrence.

06 Employee Responsibilities

All employees are trained in data protection best practices and are accountable for handling personal data securely. Violations may result in disciplinary action.

07 Privacy by Design

We integrate privacy safeguards into products, services, and projects from the design stage onward, ensuring proactive protection of personal data.

08 Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies to improve functionality, analyze usage, and enhance user experience. Users may manage cookie preferences through browser settings. Non-essential cookies may be declined at any time.

09 Google AdSense and Third-Party Advertising

BGS uses Google AdSense and other third-party vendors to display ads. These vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our site or other sites. Google’s DoubleClick cookie enables it and partners to serve personalized ads. Users can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or opt out of some third-party vendors at www.aboutads.info.

10 International Data Transfers

For visitors from the EU, EEA, UK, and US, personal data may be transferred outside your region to countries with different data protection laws. In such cases, safeguards such as standard contractual clauses (SCCs) are implemented to ensure adequate protection.

11 Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in laws, practices, or services. Updates will be posted on this page, and continued use of our services constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
